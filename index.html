Company Profile

Technology built around your business goals.

NICKOS SERVICES LTD is a professional Information Technology consultancy company committed to helping businesses leverage technology to improve efficiency, strengthen security, and accelerate digital transformation.

NSL is a full-service IT consultancy specialising in cloud infrastructure, software engineering and modern DevOps solutions. We provide end-to-end IT consulting, cloud solutions, software development, cybersecurity, DevOps and managed IT services tailored to organisations of all sizes.

We partner with growing enterprises and public sector organisations to modernise legacy systems, automate operational workflows and implement secure, high-performance IT architectures.

By combining technical expertise with a business-first methodology, we help technology investments deliver measurable efficiency, operational resilience and competitive advantage.
